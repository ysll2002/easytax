and revenue. What shipped, what measures it, and how to judge it.

**Deployed at the time of this round:** `00699ce`, `main`, built 2026-09-11,

## 1. Every page on this site has been saying its own name twice

Nobody had ever looked at the site's rendered HTML. Five growth rounds aimed at
organic search, 158 URLs in the sitemap, and every statement any round made
about our own `<title>` tags came from reading `generateMetadata` — which says
what was *intended*.

A crawl of all 158 URLs against a production build:

| | Before |
|---|---|
| Pages naming the brand twice in one title | **122** |
| …of which end literally `\| EasyTax \| EasyTax` | 119 |
| Titles longer than the ~60 characters Google displays | **150 of 158** (median 83, longest 105) |
| Descriptions longer than 160 characters | **151 of 158** |
| Pages in a duplicate-title set | 4 |
| `noindex` pages we also listed in the sitemap | 2 |

The doubling is a template collision. `app/layout.tsx` declares
`title: { template: '%s | EasyTax' }`, so Next appends the brand to every page.
Five routes appended it again by hand, and one of them is the article page —
which accounts for 113 of the 119, because the entire archive renders through
it. The title is the strongest on-page signal a search engine has, and ours
said the brand name twice and then got cut off.

**This has been true for the whole life of the site.** `/api/admin/seo-audit`
was built on 2026-09-09 to read exactly this and was never once called.

That is the third instance of one failure. The 2026-09-06 review gate had no
`days_since_last_publish`. The deploy had no way to answer what was live
(2026-09-11). The SEO audit had no caller. Each time a control was added and
the number that would show it misfiring was not. F37 is the standing fix.

## What shipped

Four of the five are traffic, which is the standing 80/20 split.

### F36 — One source for every title and description
`lib/seo-meta.ts` · 26 routes · 4 locales

`pageTitle()` strips any brand a caller appended, adds exactly one when it fits
the budget, drops it when it does not, and hands Next `{ absolute }` so the
template cannot run twice. `metaDescription()` trims at a sentence or word
boundary. Hand-shortened the 19 written-by-hand landing titles that were over
budget, plus the homepage title and description in the four locales that were
over (ja/ko/zh were already inside it).

The first version of the helper also shortened long titles at their first
colon. **Measured against the real archive, that made things worse**: it
collapsed 16 articles into 7 colliding titles — five `Capital Allowances on
Plant & Machinery` alone — because the clause after the colon is the part that
distinguishes them. Trading 119 doubled titles for 16 colliding ones is not a
fix, so the rule was removed. An over-budget title is now left whole and
reported rather than mangled.

- **Measures:** `seo.blocking_issues`; `seo.problem_counts`.
- **Baseline:** 128 blocking defects (122 brand repeats, 4 duplicate-title
  pages, 2 noindex-in-sitemap).
- **Target:** 0. Verified at **4** on this branch — the two genuine duplicate
  article pairs, which are an editorial call, not a rename.

### F37 — The crawl runs itself, and reports what it finds
`lib/seo-audit.ts` · `/api/cron/daily` · `daily-metrics`

The checks moved into a library; the admin route is now a thin caller; the
daily cron runs the crawl over every sitemap URL and stores the summary in the
day's `growth_snapshots` row, which `daily-metrics` reports with an `age_days`
so a stale audit and a clean one do not look identical.

Its verdict comes from what the crawl returned rather than from whether it
threw — a run that reached nothing reports as a failure, not a success. Two new
checks: `brand_repeated_in_title` (today's defect, named so its return is loud)
and `noindex_but_in_sitemap`, which found `/privacy` and `/terms` handing
crawlers two contradictory instructions of ours. Both are out of the sitemap and
still linked in the footer.

- **Measures:** `seo.audited_at`, `seo.age_days`, `seo.blocking_issues`.
- **Baseline:** the audit had run zero times in five days of existing.
- **Target:** a crawl stored every day, `age_days` never above 1.
  Verified: 156 URLs in 9.6s against a 300s cron budget.

### F38 — The pipeline stops competing with itself
`lib/article-quality.ts` · `/api/cron/daily-article`

Two defects at the source of the archive.

`STANDARD.maxTitleChars` was 80 while the renderer cuts at 60 — most of why 84
pages still ship a truncated headline. It now imports `TITLE_BUDGET`, so the
two cannot drift apart again.

And the pipeline had been writing the same page twice. Checked against the real
archive, **29 of 116 titles collide with another article** — 3 exact pairs and
23 sharing a subject before the colon, including five pages all claiming
`Capital Allowances on Plant & Machinery`. `findTitleCollision` refuses both
kinds before insert, counts queued drafts, and appends within a batch so a run
cannot collide with itself. A refusal is reported as the result's `error`, so a
day the pipeline declines to write says so.

Stem matching is as blocking as exact, deliberately: those five pages are
distinguishable by their subtitles and indistinguishable to a search engine
choosing which answers the query.

- **Measures:** `seo.duplicate_titles`; the cron's per-result `error`.
- **Baseline:** 29 of 116 titles colliding; 84 titles over budget.
- **Target:** no *new* collisions. Existing ones are rewritten a few a day —
  their titles now fail the tightened budget, so the upgrade path picks them up
  on its own. Verified: all 116 titles detected against their own archive,
  0 false positives on 3 unseen headlines.

### F39 — The calendar, on the page people actually reach
`app/page.tsx`

24 `.ics` fetches and 35 feed fetches in four days, against 8 human page views.
The subscribe block was on `/timetable`, the deadline checker, the
quarterly-deadlines page and the auto-signup page — none of which has had a
single labelled human visitor. Every human page view in production has been on
`/`, which did not have it.

A subscription is also the only recurring relationship available before HMRC
approval: four appearances a year, no account, no email address.

- **Measures:** `calendar_cta_click` with `placement: "home"`;
  `calendar_fetched` from non-bot agents.
- **Baseline:** 0 — the event has never fired with this placement.
- **Target:** > 0 within 7 days. Deliberately a low bar: with ~2 human visitors
  a day, anything higher is a coin flip, not a result.

### F40 — Did our pages actually reach Bing?
`/api/cron/daily` · `daily-metrics`

`submitToIndexNow` has always returned the endpoint's status and count, and
until now that answer went only into the cron's own HTTP response. Bing is half
of every search referral this site has ever had and the only engine that
honours IndexNow, so a key file that stopped resolving would have been silent.
Stored in the day's snapshot and reported as `indexnow`.

- **Measures:** `indexnow.submitted`, `indexnow.status`, `indexnow.ok`.
- **Baseline:** never recorded anywhere a person would look.
- **Target:** reported, not chased. The reading that matters is a zero with a
  403 — that is the failure this makes visible.
