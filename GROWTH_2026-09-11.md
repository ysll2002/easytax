# Growth work — 2026-09-11

Five changes against the three standing goals: traffic (the priority), trust
and revenue. What shipped, what measures it, and how to judge it.

Two things reorganised this round, and neither is a feature.

## 1. Two complete rounds of work had never been merged

`main` last moved on **2026-09-08**. PR #9 (2026-09-09, "stop scaling the
archive, start fixing it") and PR #10 (2026-09-10, "give the site a preview
card, and make its pages travel") were both built, both opened against `main`,
and both still open this morning. Nothing from either had reached `main`,
`staging` or production.

So the metrics read on 09-09 and 09-10 were read as if that work were live. It
was not. Three days of measurement judged a deployment from the 8th.

This is the 2026-09-05 lesson from the other direction. That incident was
production running *ahead* of `main` — an unmerged `claude/*` branch with two
extra commits. CLAUDE.md answered it by making the deploy workflow reject any
ref that is not `main`, which was the right fix and is still working: nothing
has deployed off-branch since. But enforcement only stops the bad deploy. It
never answers *what is live right now*, and that is where three days went.

Both PRs are reviewed and merged into this branch: `npx tsc --noEmit` and
`npm run build` pass on the combined tree, the two merge conflicts
(`lib/analytics.ts`, `app/api/admin/daily-metrics/route.ts`) were additive on
both sides and are resolved keeping both, and neither PR touches
`lib/hmrc*.ts`, `app/api/hmrc/**`, `auth.ts`, `middleware.ts`,
`DeviceDataCollector.tsx` or any file named for `fraud`/`fph`/`hmrc`.

F21 makes the underlying question answerable in one request instead of
inferrable from crawler traffic.

A 0% rate is possible. A 0% rate on forty-six people who also produced no

being people — and we currently have no way to tell. The 2026-09-08 round
removed crawlers that hit static files *by path*; a headless browser executing
our JavaScript on a real page is counted identically to a reader. F24 fixes
that, and the headline visitor number should be expected to fall again.

Two things did move, and both deserve to be said plainly:

- **Search referrals are up.** 2/week was the figure the last three rounds

- **The `.ics` calendar earned its first outside fetches** — PerplexityBot on
  09-09 and AhrefsBot on 09-09. Two distinct agents against a 7-day target of
  ≥3. Not met, but it is no longer zero, and both are crawlers rather than
  subscribers.

## What shipped

### F21 — What is actually running, reported
`lib/deployment.ts` · `next.config.ts` · `daily-metrics`

A `deployment` block naming the commit, branch and build time of the code
answering the request, plus `built_from_main`. `NEXT_PUBLIC_BUILD_TIME` is
stamped at build time from `next.config.ts` because Vercel exposes the commit
but not when the build ran, and "which commit" and "how stale" are different
questions — a correct SHA built three weeks ago is still a finding.

Two warnings, in priority order: a production deployment not built from `main`
(the 2026-09-05 drift recurring, and the more serious of the two), and a build
three or more days old (this round's failure). Verified across both, plus the
day-boundary arithmetic at 0/1/2/3/12 days. With `main` last built on 09-08,
the staleness warning fires today — which is the point.

- **Measures:** `deployment.commit_short` vs GitHub's `main` head;
  `deployment.warning`.
- **Baseline:** not reportable at all; today's answer took an inference from a
  crawler fetching an `.ics` file.
- **Target:** every future round opens by stating the deployed SHA. Zero rounds
  judged against a deployment that does not contain the work being judged.

### F22 — The homepage stops being a dead end
`app/page.tsx` · `components/TrackedCta.tsx` · 7 locales

The hero's secondary control was `#services` — an anchor to a section of the
same page. So the homepage, 39% of all production traffic, offered a visitor
exactly one way to leave it, and that way was "create an account" for a product
that cannot file until HMRC approval lands. It produced zero registrations and
zero launch-list signups in nine days.

It now points at `/mtd-deadline-checker`: the strongest thing we can offer a
stranger today, no account, works while approval is pending — and a page with 0
production views. "See what we cover" is kept as a text link below, so nothing
that worked before was taken away.

`TrackedCta` also gives clicks a `placement`, which `activation_cta_click` has
never carried outside the status bar. Counted in aggregate it cannot say which
button on which page earned a visit, which is the only form of the number that
settles a layout argument.

- **Measures:** `activation_cta_click` with `placement: "home_hero_secondary"`;
  `/mtd-deadline-checker` page views; `checker_started`.
- **Baseline:** 0 checker views, 0 checker starts, 38 homepage views.
- **Target:** ≥ 8% of homepage visitors click through in 30 days, and
  `checker_started` > 0 at all — the first time that event will have fired in
  production.

### F23 — The question people are being forced to ask this month
`app/hmrc-signed-me-up-for-mtd/page.tsx` · `sitemap.ts` · `SiteFooter`

From September 2026 HMRC began automatically signing up people whose 2024/25
return showed qualifying income over £50,000. They did not choose it, most did
not expect it, and the letter does not say what to do next. That is real demand
with a short life — it is happening now and stops being a question once the
cohort works out the answer.

A first-class route, not article 114. The archive is 113 articles and earned
ten views in nine days; a `/tax-tips` post would have got no landing-page
sitemap priority, no footer link and no canonical of its own. The page leads
with the answer (eligible for a featured snippet, the only realistic way this
domain outranks GOV.UK on a long-tail query), renders the live quarterly
deadline and the full table from `lib/mtd-dates` so it cannot go stale on 7
November, links HMRC's own guidance, and says plainly that we cannot file for
you yet — including "if approval lands after your deadline, file elsewhere and
do not wait for us."

The FAQ leads on qualifying income being **gross turnover plus gross rents,
before expenses**, because that is the misunderstanding that put most of this
cohort in the letter's path in the first place.

- **Measures:** page views; search-referred visits landing on it
  (`attribution.by_path`); `topic_hub_viewed` with `topic: hmrc_auto_signup`;
  `launch_subscribed` with `source: hmrc_auto_signup`.
- **Baseline:** 0 — the page did not exist.
- **Target:** ≥ 40 page views and ≥ 5 search-referred visits by 2026-10-11.
  Judged in 30 days, not 7: a page published today is not indexed today.

### F24 — Count people, not crawlers
`lib/bot-detection.ts` · `app/api/track/route.ts` · `daily-metrics`

`props.bot` is stamped server-side from the request's User-Agent and written
*after* `sanitiseProps()`, so a client cannot declare itself human. The
`audience` block reports humans, bots (by name) and `unclassified` separately.

Rows written before today have no User-Agent recorded, so they are
`unclassified` rather than assumed — a backfill we cannot do would be a guess
presented as a fact. The detector is deliberately conservative: a bot sending a
real browser User-Agent is counted as a person, because the opposite error
quietly deletes real readers from the one number this project steers by.

Verified against 5 browser and 10 automation User-Agents, including the
PerplexityBot and AhrefsBot strings actually in our table, headless Chrome,
`curl/8.5.0` and an empty agent.

- **Measures:** `audience.last_7d.human.visitors` vs `bot.visitors`;
  `human_share`; `bot.by_label`.
- **Baseline:** unknown and unknowable — 46 "visitors", composition unmeasured.
- **Target:** `unclassified` at 0 within 7 days (it only covers pre-today rows),
  and a stated `human_share`. Expect the headline visitor count to drop. That
  is the point, as it was for the same reason on 2026-09-08.

The strip now renders the three calculators above the product pitch, each

- **Measures:** `tool_cta_click` by `placement: "home_tools_*"`; page views on
  the three tool routes; `tool_started` / `tool_completed` in production.
- **Baseline:** 2 production tool page views in 9 days (both on the penalty
  calculator); `tool_started` and `tool_completed` have never fired in
  production.
- **Target:** ≥ 25 production tool page views and ≥ 5 `tool_started` in 30 days.
