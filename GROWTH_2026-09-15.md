## 1. The crawl that shipped yesterday spent its first day auditing vercel.com

## 2. The archive has published nothing for eight days, and no alarm was sent

Last public page: **2026-09-07**. Three drafts queued, the oldest waiting seven
days. `/api/cron/daily-article` has kept running — it wrote drafts on 09-08,
09-11 and 09-13 — but nothing has reached a reader.

The 09-13 round built the fix for this: a freeze alarm that emails the owner
every day `days_since_last_publish >= 2`, with one-click links into
`/admin/review`. It shipped in `main` on 09-14 at 06:53, before that morning's
08:00 cron.

**It has never sent an email.** The owner's inbox has two messages from
`hello@easytax.vip` in thirty days: a welcome email and a contact-form
forward. Email delivery works. The alarm did not fire.

It sat at the end of the generator, after two model calls. When one throws, the
handler 500s and nothing after the loop runs — including the notification. The
alarm was wired downstream of the thing it was watching, so it went silent in
precisely the case it existed for. F43.

## What shipped

### F41 — The crawl audits this site, or admits it could not
`lib/seo-audit.ts` · `/api/cron/daily` · `/api/admin/seo-audit`

`auditBase()` sends the production crawl to `https://easytax.vip` — the
artefact under test and the only host a search engine will ever read — while a
preview still audits itself, which is the point of running it on a preview.

`crawlWall()` is the part that matters. When a majority of crawled pages
canonicalise to an origin that is neither the crawl target nor ours, the run
reports an error instead of counts: the cron step fails, the route answers 500,
and the day goes red. One stray off-site canonical stays a finding on that
page, because that is a real defect the audit should report.

- **Measures:** `seo.base`; a `seo.error` where a wall is hit.
- **Baseline:** 156 of 156 pages canonicalising to `vercel.com`, reported as
  312 defects with no indication anything was wrong.
- **Target:** `seo.base` = `https://easytax.vip`, and `seo.blocking_issues`
  within 1 of the 4 measured here. Verified against yesterday's real stored
  payload: detected. No false positive on a healthy crawl, on a preview whose
  canonicals point at production, or on a single stray canonical.

### F42 — Four pages carried a brand they could not afford
`app/timetable` · `app/xero-alternative` · `app/coconut-alternative` ·
`app/hmrc-signed-me-up-for-mtd` · `lib/seo-audit.ts`

`pageTitle()` has done the right thing since 09-14: it appends ` | EasyTax`
when the result still fits and drops it when it does not. A route has to call
it. Ten never did, so the root layout's `title.template` appended the brand for
them unconditionally, and four public pages went out at 62–70 characters where
dropping seven characters of brand would have left every one inside 60.

`brand_pushed_title_over_budget` is the check that names this class, because
the response differs from a long headline: an over-long *headline* is editorial
and is left whole on purpose; an unwired *route* is fixable to zero and comes
back the next time somebody adds a page.

- **Measures:** `seo.problem_counts.brand_pushed_title_over_budget`;
  `title_over_60`.
- **Baseline:** 4 pages; 84 titles over budget.
- **Target:** 0 and ≤ 80. Verified over all 156 URLs: **0 and 80**, and every
  one of the 80 that remains is an archive headline — none is a built page.

### F43 — The freeze alarm moved to the cron that actually runs
`/api/cron/daily` · `lib/review-queue.ts` · `/api/cron/daily-article`

The alarm now runs in `/api/cron/daily`, which makes no model calls and has
written a snapshot row every day since it shipped, at 08:30 — half an hour
after the generator, so this morning's drafts are already in the queue.

`reviewEmailInput()` derives "what is new" from the queue's own timestamps
rather than from what a run produced, which is what lets it be sent from
somewhere that is not the generator. And a send that is not accepted now
**throws**, so it becomes a failed step and a red cron run: an alarm that
cannot raise an alarm about itself is the same bug one level up.

- **Measures:** an email in the owner's inbox; the `review-alarm` step in the
  cron's response.
- **Baseline:** 0 alarms sent, across 8 days frozen and 7 days of the oldest
  draft waiting.
- **Target:** one email on the first run after deploy, and one every day the
  freeze lasts. Verified against the live queue: depth 3, frozen 7 days,
  `reviewEmailIsDue` → true.

### F44 — A headline has to answer the query it was commissioned for
`/api/cron/daily-article` · `lib/search-queries.ts`

The check is folded into the quality gate rather than bolted beside it, so the
existing retry does the work: a headline that misses becomes a failure line the
model is handed back, in the same way a short excerpt is. The words
`coversQuery` will demand are now also stated in the brief up front, so the
retry should rarely be needed.

- **Measures:** `query_coverage.covered`; `editorial_run.outcomes`.
- **Baseline:** 13 of 41 covered, 10 of those by landing pages — 116 articles
  answering 3 queries. Same query re-drafted on 09-11 and 09-13.
- **Target:** every draft written matches its target query, so `covered` rises
  by one per new article instead of staying put. 16 of 41 within 30 days.

### F45 — A run that produces nothing says why
`lib/editorial-run.ts` · `/api/cron/daily` · `daily-metrics`

The generator already reports itself in detail and every word goes into an HTTP
response nobody reads. The only durable trace it leaves is a row in
`tax_articles` — which exists only when it succeeded. Working out why the
archive froze meant reading the drafts table, noticing four days had produced
nothing, and deriving from source what those runs must have done.

`recordArticleRun` writes the assignment, what became of it, the refusals and
the coverage onto the day's snapshot row — including from the catch, which is
the case that has no other trace. `/api/cron/daily` reads it back and merges it
forward, because otherwise its own upsert half an hour later would erase it.
No migration: the row already exists.

- **Measures:** `editorial.last_run` in `daily-metrics`.
- **Baseline:** 4 of the last 7 runs left no record of any kind.
- **Target:** a record every day, and `last_run.error` non-null on any day the
  generator throws.
