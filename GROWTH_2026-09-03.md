# Growth work — 2026-09-03

Five changes aimed at the three standing goals: revenue (£10k/month), trust,
and traffic. This file records what shipped, why, what each change is measured
by, and how to run the one-week review.

---

## Where the numbers actually were

Pulled from Supabase and `/api/admin/daily-metrics` on 2026-09-03.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Signups (total) | 45 |
| Signups, last 30d / 7d / 24h | 3 / 1 / 0 |
| HMRC connections | 15 (33% of signups) |
| Bank connections | 1 (2% of signups) |
| Filings | 0 |
| MRR | £0 — HMRC production approval pending |

Signups by month: **Apr 1 · May 3 · Jun 28 · Jul 10 · Aug 3**.

Three things follow from this, and they set the whole plan:

1. **Acquisition is collapsing, not flat.** June's 28 signups look like a
   one-off traffic event that has since decayed by ~90%. Nothing is replacing it.
2. **Revenue is blocked upstream.** Filing cannot happen until HMRC production
   approval lands, so no amount of conversion work produces revenue this month.
   The only thing that compounds today is a *reachable* audience for the day
   approval completes — and there was no mechanism to capture one.
3. **We were flying blind.** There was no events table. `profiles`,
   `hmrc_connections` and `bank_connections` row counts were the only evidence
   available, which means every step before signup was invisible and no change
   could be shown to have worked. GA is client-side only and cannot be queried
   from the server.

Point 3 is why F1 comes first: without it, nothing else here is measurable.

## What shipped

### F1 — Event analytics pipeline
`lib/analytics.ts` · `app/api/track/route.ts` · `components/PageViewTracker.tsx`

Records page views and named conversion events into a new `analytics_events`
table, and adds a `funnel` block to `/api/admin/daily-metrics`. Registration is
recorded **server-side** so ad-blockers cannot hide the one event that matters
most. Events are stamped with the deploy environment, and the funnel counts
production rows only.

- **Measures:** `funnel.instrumented` is `true`; `unique_visitors` > 0.
- **Target:** a readable visitor → register → HMRC-connect funnel within 7 days.
- **Why it matters:** this is the instrument, not the intervention. Every target
  below is unverifiable without it.

### F2 — Launch waitlist
`app/api/notify-me/route.ts` · `components/NotifyMeForm.tsx` — on `/`, `/pricing`, `/trust`

Captures an email address and a segment (sole trader / landlord / limited
company / accountant) from visitors who are interested but cannot convert while
filing is closed. This is the list to email on approval day.

- **Measures:** `launch_list.total`, `launch_list.last_7d`, `by_segment`.
- **Target:** ≥ 25 subscribers in 14 days — roughly 8× the current signup rate,
  which is the point: it converts interest that registration currently loses.

### F3 — `/trust` page, and a JSON-LD contradiction fixed
`app/trust/page.tsx` · `app/layout.tsx` · `app/page.tsx`

The product asks a stranger for a National Insurance number and bank read access
before it has filed anything for anyone, and nothing on the site answered "who
are you?". The page states plainly what is stored, what we cannot do, who the
company is, and **that HMRC production approval is still pending** — being
straight about that is the trust play, not a liability.

Separately, a real bug: `app/layout.tsx` declared a `SoftwareApplication` offer
of `price: "0"` / "Free" on every page, while the homepage and pricing page
declared £24. Google saw two contradictory Offers on the same URL. Both nodes
now share an `@id` and agree on £24.

- **Measures:** `funnel.last_7d.trust_viewed`; register rate of sessions that
  hit `/trust`.
- **Target:** ≥ 8% of unique visitors view `/trust`; no conflicting-Offer
  warnings in Search Console.

### F4 — Activation checklist
`components/ActivationChecklist.tsx` · `app/dashboard/page.tsx`

The dashboard opened on a "Self Assessment or Company?" fork, which explains
what the product *is* but not what to do next — and two thirds of accounts then
do nothing. A two-step checklist (connect HMRC, connect bank) now sits above it
and hides itself once both are done.

- **Measures:** signup → HMRC connect rate; `activation_cta_click`.
- **Baseline:** 33% (15/45) HMRC, 2% (1/45) bank.
- **Target:** HMRC ≥ 45%, bank ≥ 10% among accounts created after this ships.

### F5 — 79 orphaned articles unlocked
`app/tax-tips/page/[n]/` · `app/tax-tips/_lib/articles.ts` · `app/tax-tips/[slug]/page.tsx`

There are **109 published articles**, every one listed in `sitemap.xml`, but
`/tax-tips` rendered only the newest 30 and had no pagination — so 79 of them
had no inbound internal link from anywhere on the site. Orphan pages get
crawled rarely and rank poorly.

- Paginated index at `/tax-tips` + `/tax-tips/page/2..5` (24 per page), all in
  the sitemap.
- Per-article `canonical` — previously every article inherited the site-wide
  canonical pointing at the homepage, telling Google 109 pages were duplicates
  of `/`.
- `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD, "Keep reading" internal links, and a CTA
  (article traffic previously had no route to the product at all).

- **Measures:** indexed pages in Search Console; `article_cta_click`.
- **Target:** indexed pages 30 → 100+ within 30 days (indexing is slow — this is
  the one target that will *not* be settled at the 7-day review);
  ≥ 3% of article readers click through.

## Deploy order — this matters

**Run the migration before deploying the code.**

```
supabase/migrations/20260903_growth_instrumentation.sql
```

Paste it into Supabase → SQL Editor → New query. It is additive: it creates
`analytics_events` and `launch_subscribers` and touches nothing that exists, so
it is safe to run against production while the current build is live.

If the code ships first, `/api/track` silently drops events (returns 204) and
**the waitlist form shows "Sign-up is temporarily unavailable" to real
visitors**. Migration first, then deploy, and the ordering is invisible.

## The one-week review (2026-09-10)

```
GET https://easytax.vip/api/admin/daily-metrics?key=<AGENT_METRICS_KEY>
```

First check `funnel.instrumented`. If it is `false`, the migration has not been
run and every number below it is unknown rather than zero — do not read a
missing table as a failed feature.

| # | Metric | Baseline | 7-day target |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | `funnel.instrumented` | n/a | `true`, visitors > 0 |
| F2 | `launch_list.last_7d` | 0 | ≥ 12 (on the way to 25 in 14d) |
| F3 | `trust_viewed` / `unique_visitors` | n/a | ≥ 8% |
| F4 | signup → HMRC connect | 33% | ≥ 45% on new accounts |
| F5 | `article_cta_click` | 0 | ≥ 3% of article readers |

F5's indexing target is a 30-day measure, not a 7-day one.

The honest caveat: at ~1 signup/week, several of these will have samples too
small to be conclusive after 7 days. Read direction and the absolute counts
(especially F2, which does not depend on existing traffic converting), not
ratios computed from single-digit denominators.
