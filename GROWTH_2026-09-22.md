# Growth — 2026-09-22: the only channel that ever worked was Reddit

**This is not a build round.** Nothing shipped. It is a correction to the
record, prompted by two facts the owner supplied in conversation that no query
against this codebase or database could have discovered — and which together
invalidate several numbers that fourteen previous rounds have been steering by.

Written down because the daily agent run starts a fresh session with no memory
of any conversation. If it is not in this repository, the next round does not
know it.

---

## 2. The June spike was Reddit, and Reddit is closed now

The 2026-09-19 analysis could see the June spike and could not explain it,
because this project's own instrumentation did not exist until 2026-09-03. The
owner supplied the answer: **posts on Reddit carrying a link to easytax.vip.**
Reddit no longer permits it.

each with a decay tail:

**29 of the 41 real accounts this product has ever had — 71% — came from
roughly three Reddit posts.**

Post 3's shape is the tell. Posts 1 and 2 each produced a 7–9 day tail; post 3
stopped dead the same day. That is what a thread removed within hours looks
like, and it dates the start of enforcement to early July — which matches the
owner's account.

Per-post value: **roughly 10 registrations.** Against that, the 114-article
archive and fourteen rounds of SEO work have produced six search-referred
visitors a week and, as far as the data shows, **one** registration (§4).

## 3. The pricing change is not the cause of the decline

Worth stating explicitly because it is the obvious suspect and it is wrong.

The homepage changed from free to paid on **2026-08-25** (`3bc11dc`, English
only — `messages/en.json`), with the other six locales following on 08-26
(`58542a4`) and the standalone `/pricing` page created the same day
(`eba3dc6`). Before: `priceLabel: "Free"`, `badgeFree: "£0 / year"`,
"EasyTax is completely free to use". After: `£24 / filing`.

| window | real signups | rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1–25 Aug (before the change) | 2 | 0.08/day |
| 25 Aug – 22 Sep (after) | 2 | 0.07/day |

The collapse from 26 (June) to 10 (July) to 3 (August) had **already
finished** before the price appeared on the page. The break is June → July,
which is when Reddit stopped — not late August, which is when the price
changed. Whatever the pricing change costs, it is not visible at this sample
size, and it is not what killed growth.

## 4. SEO has produced exactly one real user, and she was a good one

On the evening of 2026-09-15 one person arrived from Google, registered, and
spent about an hour moving through the dashboard — company, P&L, banking,
reconcile, profile, HMRC — producing 108 engaged events. The 2026-09-16 round
recorded that session without knowing who it was.

It was `koomson.gifty@yahoo.com`, the only real registration in September, and
**she connected HMRC the same day**. She is the last real user this product has
acquired, seven days ago.

So the archive is not worth nothing. It is worth approximately one engaged user
per three weeks, against roughly ten per Reddit post.

## 5. What this means

The constraint was never the product's ability to convert. When 41 real people
saw it, 12 connected HMRC — a 29% rate for an app that cannot yet file. **The
constraint is, and has always been, distribution**, and the one distribution
channel that ever worked was closed in early July, two and a half months ago.
Nothing has replaced it.

Fourteen rounds of daily growth work have gone into an SEO engine on a
three-month-old domain carrying 114 machine-written articles that average 641
words, none of which meets the project's own 1,100-word standard, six subjects
of which were written five times over. That is not merely a slow channel; it is
a site profile that Google's helpful-content systems treat as a negative
signal. The consolidation work in PR #23 was the right direction and does not
change the underlying arithmetic.

## 6. What is not for an agent to do

The obvious response — resume posting to Reddit — is one this agent must not
automate, for three reasons. Posting promotional links from an automated
account is what triggers the enforcement in the first place; it violates the
platform's rules; and a bot posing as a person to market a product is not
something to build regardless of whether it works. Rebuilding a presence there
means a real person answering real questions without a link for several weeks
first, and that is the owner's time, not an agent's.

What an agent *can* usefully do is prepare the ammunition: the archive contains
114 articles' worth of genuine MTD knowledge, and turning the best of it into
direct answers to specific questions a person could post — under their own
name, on platforms that permit it (UK Business Forums, AccountingWeb, UK
freelancer and contractor groups, LinkedIn) — is legitimate work that shortens
the owner's loop.

## 7. Open decisions, none of them an agent's

1. **The standing instruction is "80% of work should serve traffic."** On the
   evidence above, the split within that 80% is wrong: it has gone almost
   entirely to SEO, which has produced one user, rather than to distribution,
   which produced twenty-nine. Changing the scheduled task's prompt is the
   owner's call.
2. **The daily report should exclude test accounts.** It currently overstates
   registrations by 17% and HMRC connections by 50%. This is a small code
   change in `/api/admin/daily-metrics` and was deliberately not made in this
   round, because which addresses count as test data is the owner's judgement
   and hard-coding a guess is how the number starts lying in the other
   direction.
3. **HMRC production approval is still pending**, and until it lands the
   product cannot file. More traffic converts to a waitlist, not revenue —
   which is worth weighing before spending heavily to rebuild distribution.
4. **`20260916_article_target_query.sql` has still not run.**
