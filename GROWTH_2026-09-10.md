# Growth work — 2026-09-10

Five changes against the three standing goals: traffic (the priority), trust,
and revenue. What shipped, what measures it, and how to judge it.

This round is about distribution rather than production. The last four rounds
built pages; this one is about whether any of them can leave the site.

---

## First: the metrics endpoint could not be read today

`https://easytax.vip/api/admin/daily-metrics` is **blocked by this sandbox's
network egress policy** — the proxy answers `403` to `CONNECT easytax.vip:443`,
for both `curl` and `WebFetch`. Nothing is wrong with the endpoint; the agent
environment is not allowed to reach the domain.

So the numbers quoted below are the ones recorded on **2026-09-08**, and are two
days stale. Everything in this round was chosen from evidence in the repository
itself, which is where the round's main finding came from anyway. Two ways to
fix the blindness, either of which would help:

- add `easytax.vip` to the environment's allowed egress hosts, or
- have the `growth-snapshot` cron (already running daily at 07:00) write the
  same payload to `growth_snapshots`, which is reachable through Supabase
  credentials rather than over HTTP to our own domain.

## The finding: the site has never had a preview image

`app/layout.tsx` declared, site-wide:

```ts
openGraph: { images: [{ url: '/og-image.png', width: 1200, height: 630, … }] },
twitter:   { images: ['/og-image.png'] },
```

`public/` contains six files. `og-image.png` is not one of them, and never has
been in the repository's history.

Every page on this site — all 157 of them — has therefore been advertising a
1200×630 preview image at a URL that returns 404. Every link that four growth
rounds worked to earn rendered as a bare text row: in Slack, WhatsApp, X,
LinkedIn, Discord, iMessage, Google Discover, and in the preview panel of every
CMS an accountant might have pasted us into.

This is the same class of fault as the 2026-09-08 finding, and it is worth
naming as a pattern: **a value that is asserted in one place and satisfied in
another, with nothing checking that the two agree.** The stale mandate copy was
nine hand-typed claims about a date nothing derived. This was an image path
nothing generated. In both cases the code was self-consistent, the build passed,
and the thing was simply not true.

Three consequences, which is why it took over the round:

1. **It wasted every link we have.** A link with no card gets materially fewer
   clicks than one with a card. We have very few links; each was worth less
   than it should have been.
2. **It reads as unfinished.** A tax product whose link previews are blank is
   one a stranger assumes is a side project. That is a trust problem, and no
   amount of `/trust` copy fixes it.
3. **It made sharing pointless to build.** There was no reason to add share
   controls to a page whose shared form looked like nothing.

So F16 fixes the card, and F17–F19 are the things worth building once the card
exists.

The judgement the last round reached, and this one accepts: 157 pages producing
two organic visits a week is not a content-volume problem. Another page is not
the answer. Being reachable from somewhere other than a search results page is.

The last round's own words: *"the next round would then have to be about getting
linked from somewhere else — which is the one thing that cannot be done from
inside this repository."*

That is very nearly right, and the gap in it is this round. We cannot go and
acquire links. We **can** build the things that get linked, and make the act of
linking possible, attractive and measurable. That is F17, F18 and F19. F20 is
how we find out whether it worked.

## What shipped

### F16 — A preview card, generated per page
`lib/og.tsx` · `app/opengraph-image.tsx` · `app/tax-tips/[slug]/opengraph-image.tsx` · `app/layout.tsx`

The dead `/og-image.png` references are gone. In their place, a generated card:
brand ground, accent edge, headline, one supporting line, an optional large
figure, and the wordmark. Next fills `og:image` and `twitter:image` from it
automatically, on every route, and it cannot 404 because there is no file to be
missing.

Two of the cards are not static, which is the argument for generating rather
than shipping a PNG:

- The **site card** regenerates hourly and states which quarterly update is due
  next, with the days remaining — today, *"58 days to the next update, 2026/27
  Q2 due 7 November 2026"*. A PNG would have frozen at deploy and eventually
  counted down past a deadline that had passed, on an asset nobody here looks at.
- The **article card** carries that article's own headline. 113 articles had
  been sharing one image with the pricing page.

Verified by fetching the rendered PNGs and looking at them: 200, `image/png`,
~60KB, correct dates, no clipped text at any title length tested.

- **Measures:** `og:image` resolving 200 on `/`, an article and a shared result;
  `distribution.share_cards.by_scraper` (below) once anything is shared.
- **Baseline:** every page pointed at a 404. Zero valid cards on the site.
- **Target:** 0 pages advertising a missing image — already true and verified on
  this commit, not forecast.

### F17 — Feeds, so the archive can be carried away
`app/tax-tips/feed.xml` · `app/tax-tips/feed.json` · `app/tax-tips/_lib/feed.ts` · `app/layout.tsx`

RSS 2.0 and JSON Feed 1.1 over the 50 most recent published articles, both
respecting the editorial review gate, both `<link rel="alternate">`-discoverable
from every page on the site.

Every distribution route the site has built so far — sitemaps, IndexNow,
internal linking — *asks a search engine to come and get the content*. A feed is
the only one that lets something else take it: a reader, an aggregator, a
newsletter tool, another site's "latest UK tax updates" block. It costs nothing
to keep current because the cron already writes an article a day into it.

The autodiscovery took two attempts and the failure is worth recording:
`metadata.alternates.types` looks like the right home for it and does not work
here, because a page that sets its own `alternates` replaces the layout's object
wholesale — and almost every page on this site sets a canonical. The `<link>`
tags are emitted from the layout's own `<head>` instead, which is what makes
them site-wide. Verified on `/` and on a landing page.

Full article bodies are deliberately **not** syndicated. The excerpt answers the
question and the article is one click away; shipping the body would hand a
scraper a clean copy of 113 pages we are trying to rank ourselves.

- **Measures:** `distribution.feeds.distinct_agents` and `by_agent`. Repetition
  is the signal — one fetch is a look, the same reader every day is a subscriber.
- **Baseline:** 0 — no feed existed.
- **Target:** ≥ 3 distinct repeating reader agents in 30 days.

### F18 — The widget, which is a backlink we can actually manufacture
`lib/embed.ts` · `/embed/mtd-deadline` · `/tools/embed` · `next.config.ts`

`/embed/mtd-deadline` is a small, framable page showing the next MTD quarterly
deadline and the days remaining, read from `lib/mtd-dates`, with one attribution
link back. `/tools/embed` is the page that gets it adopted: live preview, a
copy-paste `<iframe>`, and the terms in full.

The offer is maintenance somebody else no longer has to do. An accountant who
writes "your next quarterly update is due 7 November" has to edit that sentence
four times a year and will eventually forget. The widget does not. That
asymmetry is the entire pitch, and it is aimed at a reader no other page here
addresses — not somebody who owes tax, but somebody who publishes for people who
do.

Four details that are load-bearing:

- **`noindex`.** It is `/mtd-deadline-checker`'s answer with the page removed.
  Indexing it would put a near-duplicate into competition with the page we want
  to rank, which is the classic way a widget costs its owner the ranking it was
  meant to earn.
- **`frame-ancestors *`, stated explicitly** for `/embed/*` in `next.config.ts`.
  Framing works today only because nothing sets `X-Frame-Options`. The first
  time a security header is added site-wide, this entry is what stops that
  change silently blanking the widget on every site that embedded it, with no
  error anyone here would see.
- **No analytics inside the frame.** The root layout would otherwise have loaded
  Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics into the host's page. `/tools/embed`
  promises in writing that the widget sets no cookies on their visitors, and
  `components/SiteAnalytics.tsx` is what makes that promise true. Loading GA
  into somebody else's article is also a compliance problem we would be handing
  to the person doing us a favour.
- **An embed is not a visit.** `/embed` is excluded from `page_view` at source
  and from `isPublicContentPath` in the metrics endpoint. Counting widget
  impressions as traffic would re-inflate exactly the number the last round
  spent a change deflating.

- **Measures:** `distribution.embeds.by_host` — a list of domains that framed
  us, which is a backlink list we observe directly rather than wait for from
  Search Console. Our own domains are filtered out.
- **Baseline:** 0 — the widget did not exist.
- **Target:** ≥ 1 distinct external host in 30 days. One is not a rounding
  error here; it is the first inbound link this project has ever manufactured.

### F19 — Calculator answers that can be pointed at
`lib/share-results.ts` · `components/ShareResult.tsx` · `app/og/[tool]` · the three tool pages

The three calculators are the pages most likely to earn a link — they answer a
question a stranger is actively asking, for free, with no email gate. But the
answers lived entirely in React state, so there was no way to *point at one*.
Somebody helping a friend in a forum could link the tool and say "put your
numbers in". They could not link the answer.

Now they can. A computed answer offers Copy link / WhatsApp / Email. The link
reopens the page with the figures filled in and the answer already on screen —
server-rendered, not after hydration, so a crawler and a reader without
JavaScript both get the number they were promised. And it previews as a card
carrying that number: *"£720 in HMRC penalties, 135 days late"*.

`lib/share-results.ts` is the single encoding all of it shares — the calculator
builds the link from it, `generateMetadata` reads it, the card route renders it
— so a preview cannot state a different number from the page it opens.

Two properties worth stating plainly, because both are the kind of thing that is
easy to get wrong and expensive to discover later:

- **The card renders no caller-supplied text.** Every parameter is
  bounds-checked and re-derived through the same calculation library the page
  uses; the sentences are ours. There is no `?title=`. Without that, a branded
  1200×630 card generator on our domain is a gift to anyone wanting an image
  that appears to come from us. Junk, out-of-range and unknown-tool requests all
  404 — verified.
- **The figures never reach the address bar on their own.** They are someone's
  tax position. They appear in a URL only when the reader presses a share
  control. A calculation merely performed leaves nothing in history, nothing in
  the `Referer` of the next outbound link they click, and nothing in our logs —
  the share events record a tool and a channel, never an amount.

Canonicals stay on the clean URL, so no parameterised variant competes with the
page that matters.

- **Measures:** `distribution.shares.by_tool_channel` (the intent),
  `distribution.share_cards.by_scraper` (the platform fetching the card, i.e.
  the link was really posted), and `tool_completed` with `source: 'shared_link'`
  (somebody arrived on one).
- **Baseline:** 0 — answers were not addressable.
- **Target:** ≥ 10 share actions and ≥ 3 `share_card_served` in 30 days.

### F20 — Measuring off-site reach, which nothing here could see
`app/api/admin/daily-metrics/route.ts` · `lib/analytics.ts` · `app/api/track/route.ts`

Every number this project has ever reported counts what happens *on*
easytax.vip. That was right to build first and has now told us what it can. The
new `distribution` block counts what happens elsewhere: embeds by host, share
cards by platform, feed fetches by reader, share clicks by tool and channel —
over 7 and 30 days.

The first three are recorded **server-side only** and are deliberately absent
from `/api/track`'s allowlist. Each is evidence that something happened off this
site, and evidence a browser can forge is not evidence.

Expect zeros on the first run. A zero here is a real reading, not a missing one:
it says nobody has embedded, shared or subscribed yet.

- **Measures:** itself.
- **Baseline:** off-site reach was unobservable.
- **Target:** the block populates, and `embeds.by_host` correctly shows nothing
  for our own preview iframe on `/tools/embed`.

## Deploy notes

No migration this round — every new event goes into the existing
`analytics_events` table.

Nothing here touches HMRC code, `auth.ts`, `middleware.ts`, or any file named
for `hmrc` / `fph` / `fraud`.

`npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm run build` both pass. Everything below was verified
against a local production server, not inferred:

```
/opengraph-image                      → 200 image/png, correct live day count
/og/penalty?ty=…&fd=…&td=…&up=1       → 200 image/png, figure matches the page
/og/penalty?ty=1998&fd=nope&td=-5     → 404
/og/whatever?x=1                      → 404
/tax-tips/feed.xml                    → 200 application/rss+xml
/tax-tips/feed.json                   → 200 application/feed+json
/embed/mtd-deadline                   → 200, `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors *`
<link rel="alternate">                → present on / and on landing pages
shared link <title>                   → single " | EasyTax" suffix, canonical clean
```

Worth confirming on staging, because neither can be checked locally:

1. Paste a `/tax-tips/*` URL and a shared calculator link into Slack or
   WhatsApp and look at the card.
2. Open `/tools/embed` and check the preview iframe renders and the copy button
   works on a phone.

| F17 | distinct repeating feed agents | 0 | ≥ 1 | ≥ 3 |
| F18 | distinct external embedding hosts | 0 | ≥ 0 | ≥ 1 |
| F19 | share actions | 0 | ≥ 2 | ≥ 10 |
| F19 | `share_card_served` | 0 | ≥ 1 | ≥ 3 |
| F20 | `distribution` block populating correctly | — | yes | yes |

**F16 and F20 are already true** — verified on this commit, not forecast. F17,
F18 and F19 are behavioural and depend on people, which means the 7-day column
is a smoke test and the 30-day column is the judgement.

The number that would falsify this round: if on 2026-10-10 the share controls
have been used and `share_cards` is non-zero, but organic traffic has not moved,
then link previews were never the constraint and the constraint is that too few
people reach the pages at all — which would point the next round at paid
acquisition or at direct outreach to the accountants F18 is addressed to, rather
than at anything else that can be built here.

The opposite result is also informative. If `distribution` is still all zeros in
30 days, then nothing on this site is worth sharing yet, and no amount of
sharing infrastructure will change that.

## Not done this round, deliberately

**45 signups, 15 HMRC connections, 0 emails ever sent to them.** Carried
forward from 2026-09-08, unchanged and for the same reason: it means sending
mail to 45 real people, which is an outward-facing, irreversible action that
should be the owner's call and not an autonomous agent's. It remains the
strongest revenue lever available. Ready to build it as a dry-run campaign with
the send behind a manual trigger, on a word from Lin.

**Outreach to accountants about the F18 widget.** The widget only produces
backlinks if the people it is for know it exists, and that means contacting
them — which is the same outward-facing action, and the same answer. Building
the thing is inside the mandate; going and telling strangers about it is not.
